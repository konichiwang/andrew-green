About Andrew

Curiosity is the job.

I’m an Australian host, facilitator, educator and media maker based in Berlin. My background spans digital media, startups, investing, brand work, university teaching and live events.

I’m at my best with real people in real rooms: asking the useful question, finding the energy in an audience, making complicated ideas tangible, and creating formats where participation isn’t an afterthought.

I work in English and German, with organisations across Europe.
